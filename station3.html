The Nutritional Stress Test

Action: Demonstrate the impact of nutrient deficiency and insufficient energy.

Mechanical Requirement:

  1. Skeletal Support: One main structural pillar of your maze must be labeled as a vital mineral (Phosphorus). You must be able to remove it to show how the "skeletal" integrity of the maze collapses or becomes unstable.
  2. Energy Gate: Create a weighted gate that only opens if a sufficient "volume" of food is dropped. If undernutrition occurs, the gate must remain closed, stopping the metabolic process.

Remove structural support or accumulate enough food energy to open the gate. Drag high‑energy foods into the zone or include negative 'exercise' items to deplete. You can also click "Burn Energy" to simulate metabolic use.
