The Nutrient Divergence

Action: Model the unique absorption path of lipids and monitor how many particles take each route.

Mechanical Requirement: At the exit of the "Small Intestine," use a size-gate, magnet, or diverter ramp to ensure Lipid tokens go into a separate "Lacteal/Lymph" pipe. All other nutrients (Carbohydrates and Proteins) must take a different path into the "Capillary" pipe. The simulation now counts the items entering each path.

Drag nutrients into the divergence point; watch whether they go into lymph or blood.
