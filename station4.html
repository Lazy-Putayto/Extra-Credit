The Pepsin pH Gate

Action: Simulate the transition from the acidic stomach to the basic small intestine, tracking the substances you drop.

Mechanical Requirement: Create a "Stomach" section where a Protein bead is only released if you manually trigger a "high-acid" tilt. As the bead moves into the "Intestine" section, the track must level out or introduce a physical barrier to show the enzyme ceasing function as the pH is neutralized by the pancreas. The simulation now counts how many acid, buffer, inhibitor and protein items you've added.
