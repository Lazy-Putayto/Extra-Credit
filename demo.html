Narration Guide — Key Biological Explanations

Use these explanations when narrating the demo run:

  • S1 — Herbivore Intake: "The lipid is diverted here because it enters the lymphatic system rather than the blood capillaries due to its hydrophobic nature requiring chylomicron packaging."
  • S2 — Vitamins: "Fat-soluble vitamins follow the lipid pathway via chylomicrons; water-soluble vitamins dissolve in aqueous intestinal fluid and are excreted once plasma saturation is reached."
  • S3 — Phosphorus Pillar: "Removing the phosphorus pillar shows how mineral deficiency (hypophosphatemia) disrupts hydroxyapatite formation, causing structural bone failure (osteomalacia)."
  • S4 — pH Gate: "Pepsin activates only at low gastric pH (1.5-3.5). When bicarbonate from the pancreas raises the duodenal pH above 4, pepsin is irreversibly denatured and ceases function."
  • S5 — Divergence: "Lipids packaged as chylomicrons enter the central lacteal; amino acids and monosaccharides travel via Na+-coupled transporters directly into portal blood."
  • S6 — Microbiome: "When antibiotics remove gut flora, SCFA production stops, colonocyte Na+/water transport fails, and water rushes through too quickly to be reabsorbed."
  • S7 — Comparative Adaptor: "The sparrow’s muscular gizzard with gastroliths replaces the mechanical grinding function of molars. The hawk’s shortened GI tract reflects the ease of protein digestion requiring less fermentation time."
