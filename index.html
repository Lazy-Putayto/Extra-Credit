🅾 Project Objective

Design and build a functional, gravity-fed Metabolic Maze that physically sorts different nutrient types and simulates physiological reactions using mechanical gates. This digital version translates biological theory into an interactive web-based simulation system grounded in animal anatomy & physiology.

How to Use This Site

Each Station represents a distinct physiological process. Navigate using the menu above. At every station you can drag and drop items into the simulation zone to observe simulated biological outcomes, read scientific explanations, and test your understanding with embedded quiz questions. Data persists as you navigate between stations.

🏠 The Seven Stations

Click any station to begin the interactive simulation. Each station builds on the previous, tracing the nutrient journey from intake to absorption.

📖 Scientific Background

The Metabolic Maze models key concepts from Integrated Principles of Animal Anatomy & Physiology. Each station translates a specific physiological mechanism into an interactive simulation.

Key Physiological Concepts Covered
  • Dietary Adaptation — Morphological specializations matching digestive anatomy to diet type
  • Vitamin Biochemistry — Differential solubility determining storage fate vs. urinary excretion
  • Mineral Homeostasis — Phosphate role in hydroxyapatite and skeletal integrity
  • Enzyme pH Dependence — Pepsin activity window and pancreatic neutralization mechanisms
  • Lipid Absorption Pathway — Chylomicron formation and lymphatic route vs. portal blood
  • Microbiome Function — Commensal flora roles in colonic water reabsorption and SCFA production
  • Comparative Anatomy — Gizzard, crop, and intestinal length adaptations across avian feeding guilds
